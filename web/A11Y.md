## 2. Known limits of the automated evidence

- **jsdom has no layout, no computed colours and no real focus ring** (real-browser contrast and overflow are in section 1b). Colour contrast, zoom, reflow, the planchette's
  alignment over the grid, and what text actually looks like cannot be checked there. `color-contrast` is switched off
  in the axe runs for that reason.
- **`event.isTrusted` cannot be exercised end to end in jsdom** (it is now exercised in real Chromium, section 1b; Firefox and Safari are not). jsdom marks every event untrusted, including
  `element.click()` and user-event. The tests prove that the default check blocks every synthetic click and use an
  injected predicate to stand in for a genuine activation. A real browser must confirm that a real mouse or keyboard
  activation of Approve works and a script-dispatched click does not (see 3.6).
- **Screen reader output is not tested.** The live regions, `role="log"`, labels and the `alertdialog` are structured
  for it, but what NVDA actually says has not been heard.
- **The spirit-map graph text uses rem sizes inside fixed-size SVG nodes.** At very large text sizes labels can
  overflow their node boxes. The table beside the graph carries the same information and is the accessible
  equivalent (REQ-014).
- **The terminal grid scrolls horizontally** at narrow widths instead of reflowing, because it is a 80-column
  two-dimensional display. The chat and every other pane reflow.
