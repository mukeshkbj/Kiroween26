# Product

- This repository is a Kiroween 2026 hackathon entry (submission deadline: 4 Dec 2026, 2:00 PM PT).
- Category: **Frankenstein**.
- Concept: **Green Screen Séance** — stitch a 1970s-style COBOL / IBM 3270 green-screen system to modern AI agents via MCP, so users can talk to legacy mainframe apps in plain English (read screens, fill forms, explain batch jobs) without rewriting the legacy code.
- Audience: teams stuck maintaining legacy mainframe/COBOL systems (banks, insurers, public sector).
- Judging criteria to optimise for: Potential Impact, Technological Implementation (depth of Kiro use), Design, Innovation, Presentation (<3 min demo video).
- Prefer a complete, polished end-to-end experience over breadth of half-finished features.
